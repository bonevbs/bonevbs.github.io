## Prospective Students and Postdocs

I am looking for highly motivated and self-driven PhD students and postdoctoral candidates with strong foundations in applied mathematics, machine learning, statistics, and numerical algorithms. Both theoretical and empirical research is carried out in my group, and students who can bridge between the two — and also between different disciplines — will be a good fit.

**PhD applicants** should send an email with subject line `[PhD Application] – Name – Topic of Interest`, including:

- Academic CV and transcript of records
- Short writeup (max 1 page) on what you would like to work on and why
- Contacts for 2 referees who can speak to your ability and potential for independent research

**Postdoctoral applicants** should send an email with subject line `[Postdoc Application] – Name – Topic of Interest`, including:

- Academic CV and transcript of records
- Research proposal (1–2 pages)
- Contacts for 3 referees who can speak to your ability and potential for independent research

Informal enquiries before applying are genuinely welcome.
